## Color Grading Reference

All images should share a consistent warm tone:

- **Skin tones:** Natural warm (`#D4A574` to `#8B6342` range)
- **Background:** Neutral gradient from light gray to warm gray
- **Overall:** Slightly warm white balance, subtle contrast, no harsh shadows
